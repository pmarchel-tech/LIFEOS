# Better Future

Ubiquitous language and terms for the Better Future (ISCA) aptitude and talent mapping platform.

## Language

**ISCA Test**:
A multi-dimensional assessment tool integrating RIASEC, MBTI, DISC, 16PF, and VAKS learning styles into a 19-factor student mapping report.
_Avoid_: IQ test, career prediction quiz

**Parent-to-Child WhatsApp Bridge**:
A conversational commerce flow that generates a pre-filled invitation text via wa.me, enabling parents to send a direct test link to their child's WhatsApp.
_Avoid_: Parent portal invite

**Auto-Nudge Bot**:
A backend worker that automatically sends reminder notifications via WhatsApp to the child (after 2 hours) and status updates to the parent (after 12 hours) if the test remains unfinished.
_Avoid_: Retargeting email

**Flash Downsell**:
A limited-time exit-intent discount modal that offers the complete report and AI counselor bundle at a lower price point (e.g. Rp 60,000 for 10 minutes) when a user tries to leave the premium paywall.
_Avoid_: Refund, regular discount

**Teaser Report**:
A free basic diagnostic summary containing personality characteristics, strengths, learning style indicators, and career matches, provided to students immediately after quiz completion.
_Avoid_: Full report, free test preview
