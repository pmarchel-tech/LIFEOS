## 4. The Winning Indonesian Ad Copy Formula

Hook (0–3 seconds): Name a specific fear conversationally.
"Pernah nggak sih ngerasa..."
"87% mahasiswa ngerasa salah jurusan..."

Agitation: Validate the emotion. Don't judge.
"Wajar kok ngerasa bingung, apalagi persaingan makin ketat..."

Offer: Simple + fast + low friction.
"Cuma 10 menit. Bisa dari rumah. Hasil langsung."

CTA: Always conversational.
"Klik link di bio buat coba tes gratis"
"Tanya dulu via WhatsApp — tim kami siap bantu"
