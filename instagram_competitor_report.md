```

```
Millions of Indonesians have shared their Satu Persen test results on Stories.

Satu Persen tracks SAVES and SWIPE-THROUGH RATE — not likes.
Their carousel formula:
```
Slide 1:  Name the pain (Hook)
```

### Reason 4: Strict Visual Identity (Instant Recognition in the Feed)
- Colors: Dark navy + warm golden amber (only 2–3 colors per post)
- Font: Bold modern sans-serif (Poppins / Montserrat)
- Character: 2D doodle mascot representing emotions
- Every post is instantly recognizable mid-scroll — that is brand equity.

### Reason 5: Omnichannel Ecosystem
YouTube builds authority. Instagram builds daily engagement.
TikTok drives viral reach. Spotify builds depth.
Instagram followers come from all channels funneling inward.
